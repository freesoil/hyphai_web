# Hyphai Website Redesign — Content & Structure Spec

**Purpose:** Redesign and reorganize the site around “Adaptive Operational Intelligence for Specialty Crop Agriculture” — field-ready systems that reduce coordination overhead, improve visibility, and support daily execution without disrupting workflow.

**Tone:** Operations-first, partnership-focused, no hype. No pricing or discount language on public pages.

---

## 1. Homepage (Final Version)

### 1.1 Hero
- **Headline:** Adaptive Operational Intelligence for Specialty Crop Agriculture
- **Subhead:** Field-ready systems that reduce coordination overhead, improve operational visibility, and support efficient daily execution—without disrupting your workflow.
- **Pill / tagline:** Software • Connected Visibility • Assistive Automation • Equipment Integration
- **Primary CTA:** [ Talk to us ]
- **Secondary CTA:** [ Explore solutions ]

### 1.2 Section: Built Around Real Operations
- **Headline:** Built Around Real Operations
- **Body:** Specialty crop farms and food hubs rely on constant coordination between people, product flow, and logistics. Critical workflows—harvest movement, receiving, packing, and fulfillment—are often managed through manual communication and fragmented tools.
- **Closing line:** We design adaptive systems that simplify these workflows and integrate naturally into existing operations.

**Two-column value props:**
- **For Growers:** Movement of harvest (field → staging → packing); crew coordination and task execution; reduced walking, waiting, and manual oversight; visibility into daily field progress.
- **For Food Hubs:** Receiving → grading → packing → storage → fulfillment; lot and location tracking; exception handling and order readiness; throughput visibility and operational status.

### 1.3 Section: One Adaptive System. Multiple Components.
- **Headline:** One Adaptive System. Multiple Components.
- **Intro:** Depending on your workflow, your system may include:
- **Four pillars (cards or list):**
  1. **Operational Software** — Workflow management, inventory and lot tracking, order coordination, and operational dashboards—customized to your environment.
  2. **Connected Visibility** — Lightweight sensing and device integration to capture operational status where it matters.
  3. **Assistive Automation** — Field-ready automation designed to support repetitive operational tasks and reduce manual burden.
  4. **Equipment Integration** — When proven equipment is the fastest path to value, we source and integrate field-ready machinery into your operational system.
- **Closing:** Everything integrates into one adaptive operational layer.

### 1.4 Section: Built in Partnership with Real Operations
- **Headline:** Built in Partnership with Real Operations
- **Body:** We work directly with growers and food hubs to observe workflows, identify bottlenecks, and deploy systems in real operating environments—not controlled demos. Our focus is long-term operational value, not short-term pilots.

**Process (How we work — teaser):**
- **Headline:** Designed to Fit Your Operation
- **Steps:** Discover (map workflows and constraints) → Design (propose minimal adaptive system) → Pilot (deploy in real conditions) → Scale (refine and expand).
- **Tagline:** Modernize your operation at your own pace.
- **CTA:** [ Talk to us ]
